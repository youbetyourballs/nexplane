The Problem

Every infrastructure team has tools.
Nobody owns the change.

Terraform can provision. ServiceNow can ticket. Datadog can observe. Backstage can catalog. SOAR can automate. But no existing tool owns the change itself — from understanding and planning through approval, execution, rollback, and institutional memory. The gaps live between the tools.

🏗

Provisioning tools

Terraform, Pulumi, and CDK manage infrastructure state — but not operational changes, security remediations, or credential rotations. No blast radius. No rollback. No memory.

🎫

Ticketing systems

ServiceNow and Jira track intent. They don't execute changes, simulate impact, verify results, or maintain rollback state. The gap between ticket and action is manual, error-prone, and tribal.

📊

Observability platforms

Datadog and Prometheus tell you when something broke. They don't tell you which change caused it, why the change was made, or how to safely undo it.

Nexplane is the missing layer — the control plane that owns the change from first understanding to final audit trail.
